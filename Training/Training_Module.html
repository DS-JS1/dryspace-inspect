Site Inspection App — handover & photos

About 12 minutes. Two things to get right: passing a job on without losing it, and getting your photos off the device.

App v1.4 · Works offline · Nothing you do here is saved or sent anywhere.

01 Why this matters

Everything Dryspace quotes comes from the inspection. The proposal reads it, the quote reads the proposal, the contract reads the quote. A gap at inspection becomes a gap in the quote — and usually a phone call to you, days later, asking what you saw.

The app removes most of that risk. What it cannot do is stop two people editing two different copies of the same job, or stop photos sitting on a device that later gets wiped. Those are the two things this module is about.

02 The baton

The app does not sync. There is no server quietly keeping everyone in step. The record lives on your device until you deliberately hand it on.

One baton, never a copy. At any moment exactly one file is the live record, and it lives in SharePoint. The copy on your device is a working scratch pad.

Six stages, each with exactly one owner:

OFFICE → FIELD → TECH → SALES → ADMIN → CLOSED

Set the stage before you hand over — the stage is part of the filename, so it is how anyone can tell which file is current without opening it.

03 The handover — four steps

This changed in v1.4. The app now does the SharePoint filing itself. It used to be three manual steps, and on a phone it could not be done at all — the share sheet offered WhatsApp and Mail, not SharePoint.
  1. Set your stage and your nameTop of the inspection. Stamps the filename and the audit trail.
  2. Tap “Hand over through SharePoint”Top of the form. It uploads the record into current/ and moves the previous file into archive/ for you.
  3. Tell the next personSend a link to the folder — never the file.
  4. Then delete your device copyOnce they confirm they have it. The app will not do this for you.
The last step is the one people skip. Keeping your copy “just in case” is how one job ends up being edited in two places, with nobody sure which version the client was quoted from. Your work is backed up to wip/ automatically, so there is nothing to keep it for.
Picking a job up. On the home screen, tap Take over an inspection from SharePoint. It lists everything waiting in a current/ folder. Nobody has to send you anything.
No signal? Both buttons marked offline fallback still work the old way — and then the filing into current/ and archive/ is yours to do by hand once you are back in range.

Quick check — put the handover in order

Tap the steps in the order you would actually do them.

04 Photos

Photos used to travel only inside the draft and the report, which meant the full-quality originals lived nowhere but your device. Now they upload to SharePoint on their own.

Take photos in the Camera app, then attach them. Not through the form’s own camera. Your camera roll keeps a second copy, and the location detail survives — neither is true if you shoot inside the app.

On site, with no signal: photograph what you need, then attach each one to the right field. Nothing uploads yet, so nothing is waiting on a connection.

Back in signal — car, office, wifi: sign in once with your Dryspace Microsoft account, then tap Upload now. The home screen tells you how many files are waiting.

Wait until it says “All photos uploaded”. Until it does, those photos exist only on your device.

You cannot lose a photo by uploading. The app reads every file back out of SharePoint and checks it arrived complete before counting it as done. Nothing is removed from your device on the strength of an upload that has not been confirmed.

05 Where everything lives

One folder per inspection, holding all of it:

Site Inspections ▸ INS-2026-0142 - Smith - 12 Marine Pde, Kirra/

  1. current/One file, ever. The live record — the baton.
  2. wip/Automatic backups while you work. Never the baton. Only for recovering a lost device.
  3. archive/Every past handover, in order.
  4. photos/The app puts these here by itself. You never choose a location.
Two files in current/ means something has gone wrong. Do not guess which is right. Check the timestamp and the initials in the filename, and ask before overwriting anything.

06 When something looks wrong

“No connection” — normal in a basement. Try again when you have signal. The app is not broken and nothing is lost.

“Please sign in again” — tap Sign in. Losing signal does not sign you out; this only appears if the sign-in has genuinely expired.

“Need attention” — tap Try again. If it keeps failing, tell the office before deleting anything.

“A new version is ready” — tap Update. It only appears on the home screen, never mid-inspection.

Never delete an inspection that still has photos waiting. The home screen tells you. If in doubt, do not delete — nobody has ever been in trouble for keeping a job too long.
