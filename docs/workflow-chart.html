Site Inspection App — how a job moves through it

One inspection, six stages, five handovers. Every stage has exactly one owner at a time, and the record moves by file — there is no live sync.

01 The stage journey

Each stage completes a defined part of the form and then hands the record on. The stage is recorded inside the record itself, so anyone opening it knows who holds it.

02 The handover — five steps, in this order

Because the record lives on each device, a handover means exporting a file and importing it elsewhere. That means the record can be copied, and two copies can drift apart. One file is the live record at any moment, and it lives in SharePoint.

03 What happens to a photo

Photos are captured in the basement where there is no signal, and travel to SharePoint later. Nothing is ever deleted from the device on the strength of an upload that has not been read back and confirmed.

04 Where the record goes next

The inspection sits at the front of the Critical Client Flow. Everything downstream reads from it, which is why a gap at inspection becomes a gap in the quote.

05 Rules worth knowing

  • It works with no signal at allEverything is stored on the device and saved within half a second — every keystroke, tick and photo. Losing signal, locking the screen or a flat battery does not lose data.
  • Blank fields are normalThe form is deliberately broader than any single job. Most inspections leave a lot blank, and that is correct. The completion counts exist to find genuine gaps, not to be driven to 100%.
  • Use the installed icon, never a browser tabThe app installs to the home screen. A browser tab may not keep its data the same way.
  • Delete your device copy only after the handover is completeSteps 1 to 4 first. Deleting early, or not at all, is how two versions of one job start to disagree.
  • Long video goes via OneDrive, not the appPhotos are compressed automatically so reports stay deliverable. Video is too large to travel inside a draft or a report.
