Dryspace Site Inspection App — Changelog
========================================
v1.0.0 — 20 July 2026
- Initial release. Built from Site Inspection Form v4 (Project 2.1), all 12
  sections and dynamic reveals preserved.
- Offline-first PWA: installable on iPad / iPhone (Safari) and Android (Chrome).
- Continuous autosave to on-device storage (IndexedDB); suspend/resume;
  edit after completion; multiple concurrent draft inspections.
- Photo compression (~1600px JPEG) with thumbnails; video/document storage;
  per-item remove.
- Send: self-contained HTML report (answers + embedded photos + hidden JSON
  data block for Proposal Builder 3.0) via device share sheet; separate
  "Share photo & video files" for full-size media; all-inspections JSON backup.
- Versioning: bump APP_VER (index.html) and CACHE_VERSION (sw.js) each release.

v1.1.0 — 20 July 2026
- Office pre-fill / handover workflow: "Share draft (handover to field team)"
  button exports a DS_Draft .json; "Import draft / report / backup" button on
  the home screen imports it on any other device. Same-ID imports prompt to
  replace or keep both.
- Reports now embed raw field data (rawData) so a sent report .html can be
  re-imported as an editable inspection on another device.
- Backup files can now be restored via the same Import button.
- Dryspace logo embedded (from logo_DrySpaceSolutions_2025_alone.png) — shown
  in the app header, form header and at the top of every report; works offline.
- Drafts now carry attached files: photos (compressed) and documents/floor
  plans (PDF, images) travel inside the draft .json. Videos are excluded from
  drafts (size) and listed by name — share those via OneDrive. Drafts over
  20 MB prompt before export.
- Version footer on the home screen now reads from APP_VER automatically.
- Bumped APP_VER 1.1.1 / CACHE_VERSION ds-inspect-v1.1.1.
NOTE: backups remain field-data only. Drafts carry photos/documents but not
videos. Reports carry compressed photos; full-size media is shared via
"Share photo & video files".

v1.2.0 — 2 August 2026
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
BREAKING CHANGE TO DATA STORAGE (handled automatically — read this note)
- Answers are no longer stored against field label text. Every control now
  carries a permanent, build-time field id (data-fid, e.g. "s3.slab-design")
  which never changes, even if the label is reworded or the field is moved to
  a different section. This removes the single biggest fragility in v1.1.1,
  where renaming a label silently orphaned that field's saved data.
- Records saved on v1.1.1 are migrated automatically the first time they are
  opened in v1.2: all 296 legacy keys are mapped to stable ids, the retired
  "Basement construction type" dropdown is converted to ticked checkboxes in
  the merged wall-construction list, and its free text is appended (never
  overwritten) to the merged Other field. Anything unrecognised is preserved
  under an "unmapped:" prefix rather than discarded. Migration is logged to
  the record's audit trail and the user is prompted to review Section 03.
- Records now carry: schema (2), stage, editedBy and an audit trail.
- ACTION FOR PROJECT 3.0: report JSON now keys rawData by field id, not by
  label. The Proposal Builder must be updated to map against these ids.

Workflow, handover and version control
- New stage tracker at the top of every inspection: Current stage (Office →
  Field → Technical → Sales → Admin → Closed), Your name, last-updated
  timestamp with the editor's name, and the date the record was created.
- Audit trail inside each record: stage changes, completion, migration,
  imports and forks are logged with who, when and at what stage. The trail
  travels inside draft and report files, so history survives every handover.
- Exported files are now stamped with inspection number, stage, timestamp and
  the exporter's initials, e.g.
  DS_Draft_INS-042_S02-FIELD_2026-08-02T1430_JS.json
- Importing an inspection that already exists now shows both timestamps, both
  stages and both editors side by side, warns when the copy already on the
  device is the newer one, and relabels the second option as
  "keep both (creates a fork)" with an explicit warning. Incoming timestamps
  are preserved rather than overwritten with "now", so the comparison is real.
- Companion document added: 03_Handover & Version Control Protocol.

Form content (Section 01)
- "HubSpot deal name" added as the first field.
- Inspection date now shows the day beneath it, e.g. "Monday 20 July 2026".
- "Quote number (Quotient)" added under Inspection number.
- "Referred by" added alongside Lead source.
- Contacts restructured into two clearly separated blocks — Primary contact
  and Secondary contact — each with name, email, phone and role.
- "Property listing / plans URL" added (manual paste at the office stage).
- Photos moved to the foot of the section: "Title photo for reports (front of
  property)" (single photo, used on the front of the proposal) plus a second
  "Additional photos" field for everything else.

Form content (Sections 02, 03, 07, 11)
- 02: "History unknown" added to the better/worse/same list.
- 03: block orientation option reworded to "(street at top)".
- 03: "Basement construction type" dropdown merged into the checkbox list,
  now "Basement wall construction & material(s)" with a single deduplicated
  set of options.
- 03: "Internal ceiling height" moved up to sit with the wall information,
  under "Evidence of previous external waterproofing membrane?".
- 03: "Raft slab with stepdown for wall" added to Slab design.
- 07: Measurement schedule added — a repeating table of wall face, length,
  height, calculated area, treatment, grade and notes, with linear-metre and
  m2 totals. Feeds Proposal Builder 3.0 and Quote Assembly 4.0 directly, and
  is rendered as readable lines in the report.
- 11: video capture and transfer block — media folder link, per-clip video
  log, and upload status, with on-screen device settings guidance.
- 11: dedicated "Building / floor plans" upload plus a "Source of plans" field.

New Section 03A — Safety, Access & Discharge
- Safety: asbestos likelihood, suspected ACM locations, confined space
  classification, 13-item hazard checklist, induction and SWMS requirements.
- Access: parking, height restriction, carry distance, stairs, carry route,
  lift details, power and water availability, waste and spoil removal, site
  occupancy and working-hour restrictions.
- Discharge point: identification status, destination, distance and fall,
  existing sump/pump condition, pump power, licensed plumber requirement and
  council legal-point-of-discharge notes.
- Photo field for access route, discharge point and hazards.
- Lettered 03A deliberately so Sections 04-12 keep their existing numbers and
  no existing SOP or downstream reference has to be renumbered.

Formatting and interface
- Section headers are now white ALL CAPS on Dryspace navy.
- The Dryspace logo is pinned to the top-right of the sticky bar beside SEND,
  so it stays visible while scrolling.
- Every field label carries a reference chip (e.g. 03.4) generated from its
  live position, for unambiguous reference during reviews. The underlying data
  id is separate and permanent, so moving a field renumbers the chip without
  touching the stored data.
- Tapping "+ New inspection" now opens with only Section 01 expanded.
- The progress bar now reflects how much of the form has content, rather than
  how many sections happen to be expanded.
- Home screen tiles show Australian dates with the day name
  ("Inspected Monday 20/7/2026"), plus the current stage and last editor.
- Report header now carries stage, last editor, last-updated time, quote
  number and the app version date.

Versions
- APP_VER 1.2.0 · FORM_VER 4.1 · VER_DATE 2 August 2026
- CACHE_VERSION ds-inspect-v1.2.0

Known limits unchanged from v1.1.1
- No live cross-device sync; the draft file plus the SharePoint protocol is
  the transfer mechanism.
- Backups remain field-data only (no media).
- Videos do not travel inside drafts or reports.
- HEIC conversion: on iPhone/iPad iOS converts to JPEG at the file picker, so
  it works. On Windows the browser cannot decode HEIC, and the original file
  is stored unconverted. This is now stated accurately in the User Guide.

v1.2.1 — 2 August 2026
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Completion indicators (visual only — no change to stored data)
- Each field reference chip (e.g. 03.4) turns green with a tick once that
  field has content. Colour is paired with a glyph rather than used alone,
  so it still reads in bright sun and with colour vision deficiency.
- Photo blocks (03A and the three moisture-sign blocks in 04) turn green when
  at least one photo is attached, rather than on a field value.
- The "Longer video" block in Section 11 rolls up its nested fields — green
  once any of them has content.
- Each section header shows a completion count, e.g. 12/19, so gaps are
  visible without expanding the section. Turns green when the section is full.
- New "Highlight gaps" button in the top bar. OFF by default so the form looks
  calm; switch it on and empty fields are flagged amber. The choice is
  remembered per device. Reference chips print neutral either way.
- Per-section completion counts now also appear on the exported report, both
  in the visible heading and in the machine-readable data block.
- The progress bar and these counts are independent: blanks remain legitimate
  on this form, and the counts exist to find genuine gaps rather than to be
  driven to 100%.

"None apply" options
- A nil option added to 18 checkbox groups, worded for each group rather than
  generically: "None observed" (04 moisture signs), "None identified —
  standard precautions only" (03A hazards), "None in the work area" (06
  services), "None — ready to quote" (12 follow-up), and so on.
- Deliberately NOT added to three groups where a blank already carries meaning:
  01 Client / job history, 01 Current use of affected area, 03A Carry route.
- Each is a new checkbox with its own permanent field id — additive, no
  migration required.

BS8102:2022 waterproofing grades  (resolves a standing question)
- Section 03 replaced the BS8102:2009 grades (1 / 2 / 3) carried over from the
  Dryspace Guide with the BS8102:2022 Table 2 classification:
  Grade 1a, Grade 1b, Grade 2, Grade 3, plus TBC.
- Reference chip changed from "Guide p.8" to "BS8102:2022 Table 2".
- IMPORTANT: radio answers are stored as the option's text, so rewording an
  option would silently orphan the answer and lose it on the next autosave.
  A new VALUE_REMAP mechanism translates stored answers on upgrade:
    old Grade 1 -> Grade 1a      old Grade 2 -> 2022 Grade 2 wording
    old Grade 3 -> 2022 Grade 3 wording
  The change is written to the record's audit trail and the user is prompted
  to confirm the grade before the job goes to a client.
- Record schema bumped to 3. ensureSchema() now runs 1->2 (field ids) and
  2->3 (grade remap) in order, so a v1.1.1 record upgrades in a single pass.
- OUTSTANDING: the Dryspace Guide to Basement Waterproofing Remediation (2022)
  still shows the 2009 classification and now disagrees with the form.

v1.3.0 — IN DEVELOPMENT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Under version control from this release. The app is now developed in a git
repository; every change below is a reviewable commit rather than a folder copy.

Migration and field integrity
- Retired "Basement construction type" dropdown: choosing "Other" now converts.
  assignKeys() previously accepted only ONE legacy rule per control, and the
  merged Other checkbox already carried one, so that answer had no path across.
  Rules are now ";;" separated. Found by the new test harness, not by review.
- Media is keyed by a permanent data-mfid instead of the file input's DOM id.
  Renaming an input's id previously orphaned every photo attached to it — the
  one place v1.2's stable-id work had been skipped. Existing media is rekeyed
  automatically at start-up and after a draft import.

App updates are now visible
- The service worker no longer activates a new build silently. It waits, and
  the home screen shows "A new version of the app is ready" with an Update now
  button. Previously updates applied without notice, on the browser's own
  schedule — and an installed iOS PWA that is never fully closed may not check
  for weeks. This is how v1.1.1 stayed live while two versions sat finished.
- New "Check for app update" button on the home screen for checking on demand.
  Reports the running version and states plainly when there is no connection —
  in a basement that is expected, not an error.
- Updating refuses while an inspection is open, since applying one reloads the
  app. Only offered from the home screen.

Test harness (new)
- tests.html: 76 assertions run against the real functions inside index.html via
  a hidden iframe, so they cannot drift from shipping code.
- Migration coverage is proven against the actual v1.1.1 build rather than a
  synthetic record: all 296 keys it can produce are accounted for — 294 mapped
  to a stable id, 1 merged, 1 converted by value rule.
- Release gates: CACHE_VERSION must carry APP_VER (a forgotten bump is silent
  and leaves every device on the old app), the field id manifest must exist and
  agree, and the changelog must carry an entry for the current version.

Media handling (v1.3, in progress)
- New ds-media-sync.js: capture, naming, integrity and upload queueing, written
  standalone so the planned job-update and silica-worksheet PWAs reuse it rather
  than copying it.
- Photos now keep THREE copies: a 240px thumbnail for previews, a 1600px copy so
  emailed reports stay deliverable, and the untouched original as the evidence
  copy. Video and PDFs keep one copy.
- "Share photo & video files" now genuinely shares the originals. Before v1.3 it
  shared the 1600px copies while describing itself as full-size media.
- Every original is fingerprinted (SHA-256) when it is captured.
- Uploads are verified by reading the stored file back and comparing its size,
  never by trusting the upload response — which can report success on a
  truncated write. Nothing on the device becomes safe to delete without that.
- Storage warnings appear as originals accumulate, but capture is never blocked.
  Running out of space is recoverable; an un-photographed defect is not.
- Upload itself is not yet connected — that needs the Entra app registration.

Filenames now identify the job (v1.3)
- Photos and handover files carry a short client token, so a file that has been
  downloaded, emailed or dropped into Teams still says whose job it is:
    INS-2026-0142_Smith_2026-08-15_s4-wall-moisture-signs_001.jpg
    DS_Draft_INS-2026-0142_Smith_S02-FIELD_2026-08-15T1430_JS.json
  Previously the inspection number was the only identifier, which meant looking
  it up before you knew whose photos you were holding.
- The token is PINNED on first use. Photos uploaded before the office fills in
  the client name would otherwise be named differently from photos uploaded
  after, for the same job. A later correction to the name does not rename
  earlier files.
- The client name is used rather than the suburb because it is a real field.
  A suburb would have to be parsed out of the free-text address, and
  "Unit 3/12 Marine Pde Kirra QLD 4225" has no reliable separator.
- Capped at 24 characters so a long body-corporate name cannot run away.

Inspections are indexed by SharePoint library columns (v1.3)
- The app writes InspectionNo, Client, Address, Stage, InspectionDate and
  LastEditor onto each inspection folder, so the folder list is a sortable,
  searchable index with nothing to generate and nothing that can drift.
- Writing the index never blocks an upload. If the columns are missing or the
  write is refused, the photograph is still stored.

Fixed before release (v1.3.0)
- The "Upload now" button never appeared. The sync bar read the number of
  waiting photos once at start-up and never again, so after attaching a photo it
  still said "No photos yet" and offered nothing to press. It now refreshes when
  a photo is attached, when one is removed, and on returning to the home screen.
  Found by using the app, not by the tests - the upload logic underneath was
  correct and fully covered; nothing ever asked whether the button was reachable.

Printed guides are now generated, not hand-made
- tools/make_guides.py builds the four Guides/ PDFs from their markdown, reading
  the version from APP_VER so the filenames always match what tests.html checks.
  Bump the version, re-run it, and the release gate goes green.

Verified against the live tenant — 17 August 2026
- Sign-in, upload, verification and the library columns all ran end to end
  against the real Site Inspections site for the first time. The folder was
  created with the right name, the photo landed in photos/, and all six columns
  populated. "All photos uploaded" confirms the read-back check ran.
- The update banner also proved itself: the v1.3.0 build was picked up by
  tapping "Update now" rather than by clearing caches by hand.
